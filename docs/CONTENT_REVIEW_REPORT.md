## What every item needs

- **Cards:** a subject-matter expert checks facts, dates and the bilingual wording; confirms each source URL resolves and supports the claim; checks that the attached images and their captions suit the card; fills in `reviewer` and `lastReviewed` by publishing in the editor.
- **Images:** all attached images are public-domain (CC0) works from the Art Institute of Chicago, chosen by hand and checked to load. Captions (alt text) were written for each one and need the same FR/EN review as the cards. Cards marked *missing* below have no honest match in open-access collections yet (e.g. the Berlin Wall, Hormuz, NATO); they show the topic artwork until an editor adds a licensed photo. Event images are illustrations, captioned as such, not pictures of the productions.
- **Politics and geopolitics:** a check for neutrality and steel-manning of each position. Every geopolitics card carries a `factsAsOf` date that must be refreshed.
- **Vocabulary:** a native-speaker reviewer (required by the editor) checks the definition, register label, example, nuances, pitfalls and etymology against TLFi and the Académie dictionary. English glosses need a native English check.
- **Events:** all 15 are `[Exemple]` placeholders using real venue names and addresses. Replace them with listings from agreed sources (see `admin/sources.json`). They cannot be published.
- **Source URLs:** most point to an institution's home page rather than the exact page. Deep-link each source during review.
